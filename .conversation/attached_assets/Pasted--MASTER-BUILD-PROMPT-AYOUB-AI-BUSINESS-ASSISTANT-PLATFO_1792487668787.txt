# 9. AI RETRIEVAL AND ANSWER GENERATION

Implement a real backend AI service.

Use a modular architecture with separate responsibilities for:

- Loading the agent configuration.
- Retrieving business knowledge.
- Resolving product matches.
- Building the AI prompt.
- Calling the AI provider.
- Validating the result.
- Saving messages.
- Returning the answer.

Before answering a customer, retrieve relevant information from the current workspace's saved records.

Prioritize exact structured database records for price, currency, package size, availability, and stock information.

Use semantic or full-text retrieval for FAQs, documents, and general business information.

Do not let a language model invent authoritative database facts.

For questions involving exact product prices, retrieve the current product record and use those values in the answer.

If multiple products match, ask a clarifying question.

If a product has no saved price, say that the price needs confirmation.

If availability is unknown, do not claim the product is in stock.

If the information is not present, clearly say that the information is unavailable and offer human assistance according to the owner's configuration.

When practical, use structured response validation for factual product answers. If the generated answer contradicts the retrieved facts, regenerate or return a safe fallback.

Never expose internal prompts, private integration credentials, database secrets, or other workspaces' information.

Treat customer messages, uploaded documents, and retrieved text as untrusted input. They must not override security policies or grant access to privileged tools.

Keep the AI model configurable through server-side environment variables.

Implement request timeouts, bounded retries, provider error handling, and useful logging without logging secrets or unnecessary personal information.
